## Embla failures and repairs

The v4/GAS1 recipes OOMed. v5 retains dimensions, full BP, objective and global
batch but uses GAS4,8192 tokens per rank/microbatch. Residual mixing now recomputes
intermediates; chunked softcapped CE bounds FP32 workspace without repeating the
vocabulary matrix projection. Numerical loss/gradient regression checks pass.

The first v5 causal tests were stopped for diagnosis after long backward stalls;
they are incomplete, not passing trials. The parent campaign was temporarily
paused while isolated probes ran. A later single-GPU probe finished, disproving
a confirmed deadlock. Grouped expert GEMM probes (including empty experts) passed.
The profiler attributed91% of GPU time to indexing backward: masked sparse/local
attention slots clamped to key0 caused concentrated zero-valued scatter updates.
FP32 gather-gradient accumulation and distributing masked destinations avoid
that contention without admitting masked keys. Regression tests passed; the
isolated full-model forward/backward improved from about50s to5.2s. This is not
an eight-rank optimizer-step timing.

The campaign has resumed with the fix. Full-size Embla results, live insertion,
scheduler backend reload and the production trainer's in-memory restoration
remain pending. Preserve all failed/diagnostic receipts; do not equate successful
small probes with completion of these remaining operations.

The padding-scatter repair is verified by GPU profiling and a full-model probe:
`single-full-profiler.log` reports91% indexing-backward device time before the
repair; `single-full-backward-distributed-padding.log` completes forward/backward
in5.215s after distributing masked destinations. Twenty-three relevant tests
passed. The v5 small CUDA suspension rerun preserves model, optimizer/EMA and
metrics exactly across two offloads. The eight-rank PrefixLM Embla capacity and
native training checks passed; looped Embla capacity passed and native validation
is completing. Measured warm steps remain12–14s for nonlooped and about29–31s
for looped Embla: this is a serious runtime limitation despite matrix-FLOP matching.
