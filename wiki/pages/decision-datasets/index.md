# Decision Dataset Assessment

Start with the [DFM1-15 overview](../decision-datasets.md). This collection
assesses existing sources; it does not change training data or launch new
generation. Tables distinguish established labels, proposed conversions and
unresolved eligibility. Source counts are not usable decision-row counts.

## Methods and Coverage

* [Separate Mimir-Decide build](mimir-decide-build.md) - Owner-approved CPU
  conversions, overlap exclusions and an independent trainable corpus.

* [Individual dataset ranking](dataset-ranking.md) - Top-seven cluster expansion,
  relevance/ease scores and full pre-tokenization filesystem paths.

* [Benchmark priorities and volumes](benchmark-priorities.md) - Typed Decisions
  and Decision Index relevance, clustering and measured count semantics.
* [Volume basis](volume-method.md) - Historical overlap, inherited storage,
  sampled exposure and why version totals must not be added.

* [Coverage and reproduction](coverage.md) - Census counts, evidence semantics,
  explicit gaps and the metadata-only regeneration command.
* [Conversion recipes](conversion-recipes.md) - MCQ, grammaticality, six-way
  paragraph permutation, transformations, tools, math/code and validated
  alternatives; leakage and ambiguity controls.
* [Source inventory summary](source-inventory-summary.json) - Enumerated
  metadata inputs, checksums, evidence counts and missing coverage.
* [Searchable source ledger](source-inventory.tsv) - Exact identifiers,
  provenance and provisional conversion routes.
* [Full source evidence](source-inventory.json.gz) - Compressed JSON retaining
  task relationships and provenance for the compact ledger.
