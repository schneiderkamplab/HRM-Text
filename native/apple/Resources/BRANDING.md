# DFM Mimir branding assets

The app is branded **DFM Mimir**, matching the model-card heading.

- `DFMLogo.imageset/logo.png`: original `DFM-logo.png` linked by the
  [DFM-Mimir model card](https://huggingface.co/danish-foundation-models/DFM-Mimir/blob/2844f0178e695d7d9ce182cb660671fd34c76ce5/README.md).
  Downloaded 2026-09-19 from the model repository. SHA-256:
  `e6874acd788fc1a2979de6cf491da1e0ec988d5ebee2914c30deab9d4c7a71e3`.
- `MimirLogo.imageset/logo.png`: supplied local square, white Mimir head on red,
  copied unchanged from `~/sdu/talks/codex/NNF_follow_up_note/figures/assets/mimir-logo.png`.
  SHA-256: `cc4ea96c67190a1ea77052a16c49310f512d5b1a754c880523fa52d16095ac47`.
- `AppIcon.appiconset`: standard Apple icon sizes produced from that square Mimir
  logo using macOS `sips -z`; artwork and colors are retained, not redrawn.

Both assets are bundled offline. The DFM wordmark is displayed on a white surface
for contrast in light and dark appearances. App display names change, while bundle
identifier and saved-chat paths remain unchanged. Model license notices remain
separate from these brand assets; no additional logo license is inferred here.
