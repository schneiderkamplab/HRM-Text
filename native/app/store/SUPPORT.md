# DFM Mimir support — draft for publication

DFM Mimir runs a Danish/English language model on your device. The included model
works without an account or internet connection after installing the app.

## Common questions

**Loading or replies are slow:** model loading and generation use substantial
memory. Close other demanding apps, lower context/reply limits in Settings, or
try CPU if acceleration has a problem. Longer conversations can take longer to
process. Compaction can reduce the amount of history processed.

**Can I use it offline?** Yes. Keep outgoing requests disabled. Search and model
catalog/download features require internet access, but ordinary chat does not.

**Are answers reliable?** The model can make mistakes. Check important claims and
avoid treating its output as professional advice.

**Where are my chats?** On this device. A separately installed SDU app does not
automatically import chats from the older personal TestFlight app.

**How does feedback work?** The thumbs-up/down flow asks before sharing the current
chat and summary. The separate publication choice controls permission to publish
under CC BY 4.0. Keep your receipt ID if you later want to request deletion.
