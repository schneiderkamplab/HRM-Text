# Danish and English. On your device.

DFM Mimir brings a local language model to your phone, tablet or computer.
Write, brainstorm and ask questions without sending ordinary conversations
to a cloud AI service. The bundled model works offline, without an account.

Mimir comes from Danish Foundation Models, a research collaboration, and was
trained on SDU UCloud.

- [Get help with DFM Mimir](support/)
- [Read the privacy policy](privacy/)
- [Downloads and source code](https://github.com/schneiderkamplab/HRM-Text/releases)

Online model downloads, web search and feedback are optional. Mimir can make
mistakes; check important answers.
