## Interpretation

The new-language improvement is large versus epoch 10 and continues more
modestly after 3050K. Standard performance is flat versus 3050K and 1.88 points
below epoch 10. The DFM suite remains above epoch 10 but slips slightly versus
3050K. HumanEval declines in both comparisons. Math/code headline improvement
is dominated by BFCL, not a uniform improvement across math and coding.

The EuroEval rebound requires output-level investigation before interpreting it
as learning: English EuroEval HellaSwag is 78.75/21.719/74.141 and Life in the UK
is 68.047/31.133/63.555, whereas standard HellaSwag is
83.90/79.675/80.045. These observations do not establish an evaluation bug.

At the snapshot, Multi-IFEval ET/CA/EL remained running. The listed fixed
headline/suite populations are complete and use identical metric definitions
across checkpoints; this is not a claim that every expanded evaluation finished.
ValEU is excluded. No statistical-significance claim is made.

## Evidence and Computation

- Standard/DFM/EuroEval suites and Danish/math-code headlines use the fixed
  keys in `scripts/log_dfm5_headline_averages.py`, substituting semantic-v1 DaLA
  macro F1. All listed keys are present at all three checkpoints.
- English and multilingual populations use the `row` values in
  `logs/scheduler/dfm12_XL_epoch11_noidentity/multilingual/{epoch_10,step_3050000,step_3100000}/average/semantic-v1/population_metrics.json`.
  Their definition hashes match across checkpoints, with complete coverage.
- Epoch-10 raw roots are recorded in
  `docs/reports/multilingual_epoch10_2950k.json`.
- Later raw roots: `logs/{eval,dfm_evals,euroeval}/dfm12_XL_epoch11/step_<N>`
  and the corresponding `dfm12_multilingual` roots. Only merged metric artifacts
  are used; no shard scores are averaged as if they were full-task scores.
